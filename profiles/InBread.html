InBread

InBread is a small sandwich shop built around internal continuity. Bread is mixed, fermented, baked, sliced, and reused entirely within the same system. Nothing is outsourced.

Recipes are inherited, not imported. Techniques are passed down, adjusted, and folded back into the process. Every iteration refers to the one before it.

Operational philosophy

InBread operates on the principle that flavour improves through proximity. Ingredients remain close to their origin. Methods remain close to their source. Variance is introduced slowly and intentionally.

  • House-milled flour
  • Shared starter culture
  • Closed-loop production

Naming rationale

The name reflects the structure. Bread is made from bread. Systems reference themselves. Familiarity compounds.

Outsiders sometimes misunderstand this. Regulars do not.

Public interface

The menu is intentionally repetitive. Changes are subtle. Deviations are explained. Customers are expected to notice.
