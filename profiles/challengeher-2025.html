ChallengeHer 2025 Overnight Cycling Sportive

Status: Past event (June 21, 2025)

Event Summary

ChallengeHer is an annual overnight cycling sportive held in memory of Ann McGeeney. The 2025 edition took place during the Summer Solstice and brought riders together for a long-distance endurance challenge across Ireland.

Key Facts

  • Date: Saturday 21 June 2025
  • Start time: 21:00
  • Start location: Portadown Boat Club, County Armagh
  • Format: Overnight cycling sportive
  • Distance options: 36 miles, 62 miles, or full 100-mile challenge
  • Eligibility: Open to all abilities and genders
  • Beneficiary: Cycle Against Suicide

Route Details

The 100-mile route started at Portadown Boat Club, traveled to Dundalk Institute of Technology (36 miles), then to the Hill of Tara in Navan (40 miles), finishing at Cabra Community Sports Centre in Dublin (22 miles).
