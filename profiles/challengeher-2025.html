ChallengeHer 2025 Overnight Cycling Sportive

Status: Past event

Event Summary

ChallengeHer is an annual overnight cycling sportive held in memory of Ann McGeeney. The 2025 edition takes place during the Summer Solstice and brings riders together for a long-distance endurance challenge across Ireland.

Key Facts

  • Date: Saturday 21 June 2025
  • Start time: 21:00
  • Start location: Portadown Boat Club, County Armagh
  • Format: Overnight cycling sportive
  • Eligibility: Open to all abilities and genders
